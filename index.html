About
the work.

Ludovic Njiosseu holds a Master in Biostatistics from NYU with 5+ years of experience across healthcare, pharma, financial analytics, and consumer products. At Pfizer, he designed RWE studies that informed regulatory and commercial strategy across 5+ therapeutic areas. At FDNY/Montefiore, he built causal inference pipelines for population-level health programs. At UX Analytic, he owned end-to-end A/B testing and retention modeling for a 500K+ user product.

Every project here is built end-to-end in R without leaning on black-box AutoML — demonstrating the statistical reasoning and engineering discipline required for regulatory-grade work. Fluent in Python, R, SAS, and SQL; effective cross-functional partner to product, engineering, clinical, and compliance teams.
